# Fixed-path cost sensitivity
28 predeclared fee and additional adverse-price combinations; same completed roundtrip entry/exit observations and quantities, no re-entry or change to capital feasibility. The baseline is independently reconciled to prior net/fee/extra-slippage totals. Signed break-even one-way fee from the fixed cashflow path can be negative; that means even zero fee is insufficient under that slippage assumption. Zero observations are null, not a profitable scenario. Unresolved and rejected trades excluded and identified. Diagnostic, not counterfactual backtest or real exchange fee quote.
