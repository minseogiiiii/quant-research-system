Frozen H3 signal sink, independently replayed before simulated execution. Each of five horizons and two cohorts gets its own $10,000 initial cash; within each scenario BTC and ETH share cash. One position per product, no leverage, first timely quote, exits before entries and alphabetical product priority for same packet. No reservation before arrival; insufficient cash rejects the order, no retry. Failed exits freeze new entries and retain unresolved inventory. Regular pre-packet mark-to-mid observed grid, missing marks explicit; terminal flat return suppressed on unresolved inventory. Independent scalar accounting from event log, not an independent execution algorithm. No strategy selection or actual fills.
