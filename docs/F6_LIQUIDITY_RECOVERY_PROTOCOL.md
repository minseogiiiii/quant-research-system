# F6 first frozen liquidity recovery diagnostic
Fixed policy in code before real archive access: 1s receiver grid, 10 prior fresh L1 bid-size samples, depletion at 50% of median and recovery to 80% of frozen baseline with bid size > ask size, long-only spot; 5s hold after indicative entry, 100ms target delay, 1500ms tolerance, 50% displayed depth, assumed 10bps one-way fee and 2bps adverse price adjustment. No later favorable retry; unresolved exit stops new entries and suppresses flat cash. This is an engineering hypothesis test and not an executable alpha. Cash baseline only; no annualized Sharpe from irregular trade returns. No model selection. Real archived and separately collected data are diagnostic until adequate chronological untouched samples, independent strategy/fill witness and cost calibration exist.
